# IRSTD 2025–2026 research scan

Search date: 2026-09-15

## Verified trends and representative sources

- Evaluation/generalization: NeurIPS 2025, *Rethinking Evaluation of Infrared Small Target Detection* — hybrid pixel/target metrics, failure analysis, cross-dataset evaluation. https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/a81051ae2c8b1e46bd51480917b8ab84-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html
- Cross-domain learning: *Rethinking Generalizable Infrared Small Target Detection* — RealScene-ISTD, cross-view alignment, target/background fusion, noise-guided representation learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.16487
- Vision-language/foundation models: CVPR 2025 SAIST. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Zhang_SAIST_Segment_Any_Infrared_Small_Target_Model_Guided_by_Contrastive_CVPR_2025_paper.html
- Text-guided IRSTD: ICCV 2025 Text-IRSTD. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Huang_Text-IRSTD_Leveraging_Semantic_Text_to_Promote_Infrared_Small_Target_Detection_ICCV_2025_paper.html
- Weak supervision/active learning: ICCV 2025, *From Easy to Hard: Progressive Active Learning Framework for Infrared Small Target Detection with Single Point Supervision*. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Yu_From_Easy_to_Hard_Progressive_Active_Learning_Framework_for_Infrared_ICCV_2025_paper.html
- Shape-aware operators/loss: AAAI 2025 pinwheel convolution and scale-based dynamic loss. https://doi.org/10.1609/AAAI.V39I9.32996
- Foundation-driven efficient transfer: FDEP, frozen VFM priors plus implicit self-distillation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05511
- Moving targets/foundation model: CVPR 2026 Findings IRDINO, DINOv3 adaptation plus second-order motion awareness. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026F/html/Xu_IRDINO_Adapting_DINOv3_with_Second-Order_Motion_Awareness_for_Moving_Infrared_CVPRF_2026_paper.html
- Noise suppression: CVPR 2026, *Seeing Through the Noise*. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Yuan_Seeing_Through_the_Noise_Improving_Infrared_Small_Target_Detection_and_CVPR_2026_paper.html
- Physics-aligned adaptive filtering: NUDTNet, Gaussian/matched-filter-inspired content-adaptive operators. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.infrared.2025.106082
- Synthetic data: ISTDiffuser, conditional diffusion generation for infrared small-target imagery. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.optlastec.2025.113557
- Unsupervised moving-target domain adaptation: SCORE, style-swap consistency and domain-aware refinement. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.infrared.2026.106686

## Synthesis

Crowded directions: another U-Net/attention/Mamba block; generic frequency enhancement; simple CLIP/SAM fusion; reporting only same-dataset mIoU.

Open opportunities: inverse imaging and sensor-aware generative modeling; continuous/sub-pixel representation; calibrated false-alarm control; background-only foundation learning; causal/test-time sensor adaptation; adversarial but physics-constrained clutter generation; unified segmentation/localization/tracking under operational risk.
